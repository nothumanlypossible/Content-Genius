## Brand Voice Notes

- **Tone:** Proud, direct, community-driven. Not corporate. Speak like someone who respects the Bay.
- **Avoid:** Generic seafood marketing clichés ("ocean-fresh!"). Over-explaining the tech.
- **Lean into:** The injustice of the old model. The heritage of the waterman. The simplicity of buying direct.
- **CTA default:** "Link in bio" on Instagram. Direct link on Twitter/X.
