# Crabeezy — Launch Strategy

**Type:** Two-sided marketplace pre-launch → full launch
**Channels (ORB framework):** Owned (email list), Rented (Facebook, Instagram), Borrowed (local media, dock influencers)
**Strategic constraint:** Supply must precede demand. Do not open the buyer-facing map until 10–15 active watermen have listings.

---

## Phase Map

```
Phase 1: Supply Seeding        → Target: 10–15 watermen active
Phase 2: Buyer Warm-Up         → Target: 500+ waitlist, community activated
Phase 3: Soft Launch           → Waitlist buyers only, live map open
Phase 4: Full Launch           → Public, all channels, full push
Phase 5: Post-Launch           → Waterman spotlight cadence, first reviews
```

---

## Phase 1 — Supply Seeding (Start Now, Run Until 15 Watermen Active)

**Goal:** Get 10–15 verified watermen with active storefronts before a single buyer can open the map.

**Actions:**
- Execute waterman recruitment campaign (see `waterman-recruitment.md`)
- Personally onboard every early waterman — call them, walk them through setup, make sure their first listing goes live
- Photograph or get photos from each waterman: face, boat, catch. You need these for Phase 2 content.
- Collect one quote from each waterman about why they joined or what they think of the traditional wholesale model
- Set the milestone gate: when Waterman #15 goes live, flip to Phase 3

**What you're NOT doing yet:**
- Running paid ads
- Publicly launching the app on social
- Sending the launch email to your buyer list

**Content during Phase 1:**
- Tease posts only — "Something is coming" style. No screenshots, no map, no features yet.
- Waterman behind-the-scenes content (photos from the dock, early morning pull videos, short stories)
- Goal: build intrigue, not reveal everything

---

## Phase 2 — Buyer Warm-Up (Run Simultaneously with Phase 1)

**Goal:** Build the waitlist to 500+ genuinely activated buyers before Phase 3.

**Actions:**

### Email
- Launch the referral waitlist mechanic (see `referral-waitlist.md`)
- Send the waitlist email sequence (see `email-waitlist-sequence.md`)
- Keep existing waitlist warm with a "progress update" email every 7–10 days:
  - "We now have [X] watermen. Getting close."
  - "Here's a photo of [Waterman Name] who just joined."

### Facebook (primary rented channel)
- Post in local Maryland community groups (Anne Arundel, Baltimore County, Calvert, St. Mary's, Talbot, Dorchester)
- Post in Maryland foodie/food groups
- Post in Chesapeake Bay and crabbing groups
- Use templates from `facebook-launch-content.md`

### Instagram / TikTok
- Behind-the-scenes dock content: trap pulls, morning on the water, waterman portraits
- Use templates from `launch-social-content.md`
- No product screenshots yet — all "feel" content

### Borrowed channels — identify now
1. **Local media:** Pitch to [Bay Times, Capital Gazette, Southern Maryland Chronicle, WBAL, WTOP] — angle: "Solo founder builds app to help Chesapeake Bay watermen cut out the wholesaler." This is a genuinely good local story.
2. **Food bloggers / local Instagrammers:** Maryland food accounts that cover local sourcing. Offer first access + a dock experience.
3. **Dock influencers:** Any waterman with a YouTube channel or active social presence. One authentic dock video from a real waterman outperforms any ad.
4. **Maryland crabbing/seafood podcasts or YouTube channels:** Pitch an interview about the platform.

### Media pitch (journalist outreach)

**Email subject:** Solo founder builds app for Chesapeake Bay watermen to cut out the wholesaler

**Pitch body:**
> Hi [Name],
>
> I'm a solo founder from [location] who spent 8 months building Crabeezy — a marketplace app that lets Maryland watermen sell their catch directly to buyers, cutting out the wholesale intermediary entirely.
>
> The angle I think your readers would find interesting: watermen using DNR commercial licenses have been locked into a pricing model that pays them 15–20% less than direct-to-consumer pricing for decades. They have no digital presence, no buyer discovery layer, and no way to take pre-orders. I built an app to change that.
>
> It has a live map of fresh hauls, DNR license verification on every seller, and Stripe-powered same-day payouts for watermen.
>
> I'm about to launch and would love to offer you an exclusive look before we go public — including a dock visit with one of our first watermen if you're interested.
>
> Happy to talk anytime.
> [Your name] | [phone]

## Phase 3 — Soft Launch (Waitlist-Only Access)

**Trigger:** 15 watermen active + waitlist at 500+

**Goal:** Controlled launch to validate the buyer experience before going fully public.

**Actions:**
- Send Email 5 of the waitlist sequence ("You're getting access in 48 hours")
- Open app download (App Store + Google Play)
- Invite waitlist in batches — start with first 100, monitor for issues, then open the rest
- Go live on Facebook with a dock Facebook Live event (see `facebook-launch-content.md` for the Live playbook)
- Post launch announcement across all social channels

**What to watch:**
- Are buyers actually completing orders, or just browsing?
- Are watermen getting notifications and fulfilling orders?
- Any friction in the payment flow?
- Response time for support questions

**Soft launch messaging:**
- Frame as "early access for our waitlist" — this is a feature, not a limitation
- "You asked for this. Here it is." — emphasize that it happened because of community interest

## Phase 4 — Full Public Launch

**Trigger:** 3–5 days after soft launch, if things are working

**Goal:** Maximum visibility. Open signups to everyone. Full channel push.

**Actions:**

### Launch Day Timeline

| Time | Action |
|------|--------|
| 6:00am | Go live from a waterman's dock on Facebook Live |
| 8:00am | Send launch email to full list ("Crabeezy is live — download now") |
| 9:00am | Post launch announcement on Facebook Page, Instagram, TikTok |
| 10:00am | Engage every comment — respond to everyone in the first hour |
| 12:00pm | Post a midday update — "X orders placed in the first 4 hours" |
| 3:00pm | Share in all Maryland Facebook community groups |
| 6:00pm | Evening update — real-time stats, thank you post |

### Launch Announcement — Facebook Page (launch day)

> 🦀 Crabeezy is live.
>
> Open the app. Find fresh Maryland crabs direct from the waterman who pulled them this morning. No middleman. No warehouse. No guessing.
>
> [X] verified watermen. Live map. Direct orders. Same-day catch.

### Product Hunt (optional — secondary push)

Product Hunt works best for tech-savvy audiences. For Crabeezy, local channels outperform PH significantly. However, if you want a PH push:
- Schedule for a Tuesday or Wednesday (higher traffic days)
- Get 10–15 friends/supporters to upvote and leave genuine comments first thing in the morning
- Tagline: "Buy fresh Maryland crabs direct from the waterman who caught them today"
- Short demo video: open app → see map → find listing → order

**Verdict:** Do local launch first. Add Product Hunt 2–3 weeks later as a secondary awareness push, not the primary launch moment.

## Phase 5 — Post-Launch (First 30 Days)

**Goal:** Sustain momentum, activate repeat buyers, keep onboarding new watermen

**Actions:**
- Weekly waterman spotlight post (Facebook + Instagram)
- Weekly "fresh haul alert" post — what's available this week from which watermen
- Follow up with every buyer who placed a first order — ask for feedback, ask for a review
- Monitor which watermen have the most orders — feature them prominently
- Reach out to any waterman who signed up but hasn't posted a listing — offer personal help
- Run a "tag someone who would love fresh crabs" engagement post on Facebook
- Keep onboarding new watermen — the map should grow every week

**Metrics to track post-launch:**
- Watermen active (listed at least one haul this week)
- Buyers who placed 2+ orders (repeat purchase rate)
- New waterman sign-ups per week
- App downloads vs. orders placed (conversion rate)
- Average order value

## Launch Assets Checklist

### Ready Before Phase 3 (Soft Launch)

- [ ] App live on App Store and Google Play
- [ ] Landing page updated with download links (not just waitlist form)
- [ ] 15 watermen with active storefronts and at least one listing each
- [ ] Waterman photos for social content (at least 5–10 waterman portrait shots)
- [ ] Email sequences set up in your email tool and tested
- [ ] Referral waitlist mechanic live and tested
- [ ] Facebook Live equipment ready (phone, stabilizer, good audio if possible)
- [ ] Support channel ready (email or chat) for day-one questions

### Ready Before Phase 4 (Full Launch)

- [ ] Launch day post copy prepared for all platforms
- [ ] Launch email drafted and scheduled (T-24 hours)
- [ ] Media pitches sent (T-7 days minimum)
- [ ] Facebook groups identified for day-of sharing
- [ ] Tracking in place (Supabase events or GA4 to measure downloads and orders)

## ORB Channel Summary

| Channel | Type | Priority | What It Does |
|---------|------|----------|--------------|
| Email waitlist | Owned | #1 | Direct access to most interested buyers |
| Facebook (page + groups) | Rented | #2 | Community reach, waterman Facebook Live events |
| Instagram / TikTok | Rented | #3 | Visual storytelling, behind-the-scenes |
| Local media | Borrowed | #4 | Credibility + reach to non-social audiences |
| Food bloggers / influencers | Borrowed | #5 | Trust + visual content you can reshare |
| Word of mouth from watermen | Borrowed | #6 | Highest trust signal in waterman community |

**Keep every rented and borrowed channel pointing back to owned:** email signup or app download. That's the only asset you fully control.
