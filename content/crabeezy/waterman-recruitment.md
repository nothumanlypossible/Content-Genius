# Crabeezy — Waterman Recruitment Campaign

**Goal:** Sign up 10–20 active watermen before the buyer-facing launch
**Why this comes first:** Without supply on the map, buyers land on an empty screen and churn. The waterman side must be seeded before the buyer push goes wide.

---

## Channels for Reaching Watermen

Watermen are not on LinkedIn. They are on:

1. **Facebook** — primary. They're in local crabbing and watermen groups, buy/sell groups, and community pages. Templates below.
2. **Word of mouth** — highest trust. One waterman telling another carries more weight than any ad. Ask every early adopter to recruit one person they know.
3. **Marine supply shops and bait shops** — physical locations where watermen gather. Leave printed one-pagers (template below) or ask the owner to spread the word.
4. **Maryland DNR** — Commercial crabbing licenses are public record. This data can be requested via FOIA from the Maryland Department of Natural Resources. This is a direct list of every licensed commercial crabber in the state.

## Recruiting Milestones

| Milestone | Action |
|-----------|--------|
| 5 watermen signed up | Begin soft promotion in local buyer Facebook groups |
| 10 watermen signed up | Send Email #3 of the waitlist sequence to buyers ("We now have 10 verified watermen") |
| 15 watermen signed up | Begin full buyer push — flip the launch switch |
| 20 watermen signed up | Run Facebook Live dock event, major launch announcement |

**Do not open the map to buyers until at least 10–15 active watermen have listings.** An empty map kills trust and doesn't recover.
