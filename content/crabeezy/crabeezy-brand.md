- **Footer copy:** "Made with ❤️ for the bay"

## What It Is
Crabeezy is a mobile marketplace app connecting buyers directly with verified local Maryland blue crab sellers (watermen, crab shacks, seafood markets) — cutting out the middleman for fresher crabs at lower prices.

---

## Key Value Props

### For Buyers
- Up to **40% savings** vs. retail
- **0% buyer fees** (platform fee of $3 + 2.9% Stripe processing paid by buyer)
- **Same-day fresh pickup**
- Verified sellers with ratings & reviews
- 100% local Maryland watermen
- GPS-based seller discovery
- Secure checkout via Stripe (all major cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay)
- Order notifications, save favorites, buyer protection

### For Sellers
- **0% seller commission** — sellers keep 100% of listed price
- No monthly fees, no hidden charges, no listing fees
- Free: seller verification, inventory management tools, order notifications
- Stripe payouts directly to bank account
- Verified badge for credibility
- Real-time inventory management

## Products / Features

### Main App
- Browse verified local crab sellers by GPS
- Real-time inventory, prices, reviews
- Secure Stripe checkout
- Order status notifications
- Pickup scheduling

### Dockside Reserve (Bulk Orders)
- Orders up to **100 bushels**
- Multi-seller fulfillment (multiple crabbers fill one order)
- Recurring order scheduling (weekly, bi-weekly, monthly)
- Wholesale pricing requests
- Priority flagging for urgent orders
- Real-time fulfillment tracking
- **Target audiences:**
  - Restaurants & Markets (recurring wholesale)
  - Events & Catering (weddings, festivals, corporate, fundraisers)
  - Family & Friends (cookouts, gatherings, seasonal stock-up)

## How It Works (Buyers)
1. Download the app → create free account
2. Browse verified local sellers nearby
3. Place order, select pickup time, pay securely
4. Get notified when ready → pick up fresh

## How It Works (Sellers)
1. Apply via app
2. Get verified (within 48 hours)
3. Set up storefront (products, prices, pickup hours)
4. Go live & receive orders
