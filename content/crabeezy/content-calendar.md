# Crabeezy — 30-Day Pre-Launch Content Calendar

**Phase:** Pre-launch (supply seeding + buyer warm-up)
**Primary channel:** Facebook (page + groups)
**Supporting channels:** Instagram, TikTok, email
**Goal:** Build brand awareness, recruit watermen, grow waitlist to 500+

---

## How to Use This Calendar

- **Facebook post** = post on the Crabeezy Facebook Page
- **Facebook group** = post in Maryland community/crabbing/foodie groups (rotate through target groups, don't repeat a group more than 1x per week)
- **Instagram** = static image or Reel
- **Email** = send to waitlist (tied to email sequence)
- Content marked with `[source]` links to the full copy in another file

**Scheduling tool:** Use Buffer (free tier) to schedule Facebook and Instagram posts in advance. Batch a week at a time every Sunday.

## Tools for Scheduling and Publishing

| Tool | What it does | Cost |
|------|-------------|------|
| Buffer | Schedule Facebook + Instagram posts in bulk | Free tier (3 channels, 10 posts/channel) |
| Meta Business Suite | Schedule Facebook and Instagram natively (free) | Free |
| TikTok Creator Studio | Schedule TikTok posts | Free |
| Mailchimp / Kit / Resend | Schedule email sequence | Free tiers available |

**Recommendation for a solo founder:** Use Meta Business Suite (free) for Facebook and Instagram scheduling. Use your email platform's automation for the waitlist sequence. Keep it simple — don't add tools complexity before launch.
