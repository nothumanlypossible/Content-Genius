## Facebook Strategy Overview

Facebook is the primary battleground for Crabeezy because **this is where the transaction already exists** — in local buy/sell groups, community pages, and word-of-mouth posts. The strategy is two-track:

1. **Crabeezy Facebook Page** — Build the brand, post consistently, use Live video
2. **Local Group Infiltration** — Post directly into Maryland seafood, community, and buy/sell groups where buyers and watermen already gather

> Key rule: Never put the app link in the post body. Facebook kills reach on posts with external links. Put it in the **first comment** instead.

## Facebook Live Playbook

Facebook Live is the highest-reach content format on the platform. For Crabeezy, Live video from the dock is a natural fit — it's authentic, local, and shows the product in its most compelling context.

### Live Concept 1: "Morning Pull" with a Waterman
**Setup:** Go live with a waterman as he checks his traps at dawn or early morning.

**Script outline:**
- Intro: "Good morning — we're out on the Bay with [Name] in [Location]. He's been doing this for [X] years."
- Show the trap pull: "This is what a fresh haul looks like before it ever touches a wholesaler."
- Walk through the Crabeezy app listing live: "Watch how he puts this on the app right now — sets his price, his quantity, his pickup window."
- End: "If you're watching this and you want today's catch, the link is in the comments. This is buying local."

---

### Live Concept 2: "Dock to Table" Buyer Experience
**Setup:** Go live following a buyer who uses the app to find and pick up crabs from a nearby waterman.

**Script outline:**
- Open app on screen, show the live map
- Find a nearby listing, tap through the order flow
- Drive to the dock, meet the waterman
- Show the handoff: "This is [Name]. We found him on Crabeezy this morning."
- Close: "Bought today. On the table tonight. That's dock-to-dish."

---

### Live Concept 3: "Steamed to Order" at the Dock
**Setup:** Live from a waterman who steams on-site. Show the order coming in through Crabeezy, the steaming process, and the pickup.

**Best for:** Late morning / lunchtime on a weekend. Maximum local food traffic.

## Brand Voice on Facebook

- **Speak like a neighbor, not a brand.** Facebook rewards community tone. Avoid corporate language.
- **Tell stories.** A waterman's name and location outperforms any feature description.
- **Ask questions.** Facebook's algorithm rewards comments. End most posts with a question or a "tag someone" prompt.
- **Never lead with the link.** Always put the CTA in the first comment.
- **Engage every comment.** Reply to everyone, especially in the first hour after posting. Early engagement determines reach.
